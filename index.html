Expert Tips

Keep Your Appliances Healthy

Simple habits that extend the life of your kitchen appliances.

1
Clean Chimney Filters Monthly

Remove and soak baffle filters in warm soapy water every 30 days to prevent grease build-up. This maintains suction and motor life.

2
Keep Hob Burners Dry

After cooking, dry the burner caps and rings with a cloth. Moisture causes clogging and affects ignition over time.

3
Check Gas Rubber Pipes Yearly

Rubber gas pipes harden and crack over time. Get them inspected by a professional once a year to prevent leaks.

4
Change RO Filters on Schedule

Pre-filters should be changed every 3–6 months and membranes every 12–18 months for safe, clean drinking water. AMC ensures this is never missed.

5
Don't Ignore Unusual Sounds

If your chimney motor or purifier makes unusual noises, call us early. Catching issues early saves you repair cost and prevents full breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what customers ask us most often.

What is included in the AMC plan?
Our Annual Maintenance Contract includes 3 scheduled service visits per year — approximately one every 4 months. Each visit includes full inspection, deep cleaning and complete servicing. Spare parts are charged separately at cost price. We also send you reminders when your next visit is due.
What is the difference between One-Time and AMC service?
A One-Time visit is a single service call — complete service + deep cleaning, all included in one visit. An AMC plan covers 3 visits spread over 12 months with added benefits like priority scheduling, service reminders and spare parts at cost price. AMC gives far better value for year-round upkeep.

Service pricing depends on the specific appliance brand, model, type of problem and parts required. A fixed price online would either be too high or not cover your actual job. We prefer to give you an honest, personalised quote after understanding your exact situation. Call or WhatsApp us — it takes 2 minutes.

We carry the most common spare parts for all major brands in our service vehicle. For rare or specific parts, we will source genuine parts and inform you of the cost before ordering. You never have to arrange parts yourself.
Can you service brands not listed on your site?
We cover all major Indian and imported kitchen appliance brands. The list on our site is not exhaustive. Call us at 9773590055 to quickly confirm if we service your specific brand and model — in most cases, we can.
How soon can I get a technician?
For most areas in Mumbai Central, we can arrange a same-day visit if you call before 2 PM. Otherwise, next-day slots are almost always available. AMC customers get priority scheduling. We confirm your appointment within 1–2 hours of booking.
Do you provide a warranty on repairs?
